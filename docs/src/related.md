# Related projects

## Microclimates

- Ilya Maclean's [MICROCLIM hub](https://sites.google.com/view/microclim/)

## Climate and land-surface models

- [CliMA](https://clima.caltech.edu/) ([GitHub](https://github.com/CliMA)), the Climate Modeling Alliance's Earth system model in Julia
- [ClimaLand.jl](https://clima.github.io/ClimaLand.jl/stable/), CliMA's land model: soil, snow, canopy and their coupling to the atmosphere
- [ClimaAtmos.jl](https://clima.github.io/ClimaAtmos.jl/dev/), CliMA's atmosphere model

## Dynamic Energy Budget theory

- [Add-my-Pet](https://www.bio.vu.nl/thb/deb/deblab/add_my_pet/), DEB parameters for thousands of species
- [DEBportal](https://debportal.debtheory.org/)
- [DynamicEnergyBudgets.jl](https://github.com/rafaqz/DynamicEnergyBudgets.jl) and [DEBScripts](https://github.com/rafaqz/DEBScripts), DEB plant models coupled to microclimates in Julia

## Mechanistic niche modelling

- [NicheMapR](https://mrke.github.io/), the R package this ecosystem succeeds
- [The TrEnCh Project](https://trenchproject.github.io/)
