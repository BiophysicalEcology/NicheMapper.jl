Architecture

🌦️ Microclimates

Soil, surface and air conditions from Microclimate.jl, run over points and grids from any forcing data set with MicroclimateMapper.jl.

🌬️ Physics of the environment

Properties of air and water from FluidProperties.jl, and clear-sky solar radiation from SolarRadiation.jl.

🔥 Heat and water exchange

Body and leaf temperatures from HeatExchange.jl, for shapes and insulation from BiophysicalGeometry.jl.

🦎 Behaviour and physiology

Thermoregulation and activity with BiophysicalBehaviour.jl, thermal performance with ThermalPhysiology.jl.

⚖️ Metabolism and scaling

Dynamic Energy Budget life cycles with DEBtool_J.jl, and allometry with BiologicalScaling.jl.

🗺️ Animals, plants and microbes

The AnimalMapper, PlantMapper and MicrobeMapper packages (planned) put organisms into mapped microclimates.

📏 Units, dispatch and speed

Every package uses Unitful.jl, swaps sub-models by passing a type, and aims to be type-stable, non-allocating and differentiable.

What it does ​

Starting from the functional traits of an organism, the packages compute the sequence of environments it experiences in a habitat, and whether that sequence lies inside or outside its niche. This integrates:

  • the external exchange of heat and water, from biophysical ecology;

  • the internal processes of growth, development and reproduction, from metabolic theory (Dynamic Energy Budget theory);

  • the microclimates the organism is exposed to, from micrometeorology, soil physics and hydrology.

See History for how this grew from a question about why desert iguanas change colour.

How to install NicheMapper.jl? ​

NicheMapper.jl is a meta-package: installing it pulls in the whole ecosystem. It is not yet registered, so install it from GitHub:

julia
julia> using Pkg
julia> Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/BiophysicalEcology/NicheMapper.jl")

Each package can also be installed on its own; see The packages for where to start for a given problem, and For NicheMapR users for where NicheMapR's functions now live.

Status ​

The physics and biology packages are in use; the Mapper packages for animals, plants and microbes are at the design stage. The Architecture overview sets out the design, the use cases it serves and the build order.
